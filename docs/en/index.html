Features

Three station shortcuts
Save your favorite stations on three buttons. Switch from the system tray menu too.
Polish station catalog
Search the included MP3 station catalog by name or town. Station availability depends on the broadcaster.
Music when you sign in
Enable autostart to play radio in the system tray after you sign in to Windows.
Track titles
See the artist and track title when the station provides them.
Polish and English
Switch PL/EN in About or in the system tray menu.
Verified updates
Before installation, the app verifies the author’s signature and the file checksum.

Privacy and connections

Your stations, volume and language are stored in your Windows account. Radio connects to the selected station for audio and track information. The broadcaster receives your IP address and may include advertising in the stream. On startup and on request, radioMAT checks GitHub releases; the installer downloads only when you choose to update. The coffee, website and report links open your browser.

The app has no accounts or telemetry. The Radio Browser catalog is included in the installation. Open source under the MIT license.
