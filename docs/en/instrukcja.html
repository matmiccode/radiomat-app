Guide radioMAT

Listening and shortcuts

Click a station. “More stations” opens the catalog. Search, select a station, then choose “Listen to selected station”. Buttons 1–3 assign it to a shortcut.

System tray and autostart

Closing the window keeps music playing. Double-click the tray icon to return. Right-click it for station shortcuts and a vertical volume slider. A checked “Mute radio” box means playback is stopped; uncheck it to resume at the previous volume. “Exit radioMAT” at the bottom closes the app. “Play when Windows starts” starts radio after you sign in.

Language and information

Open “About”, then PL/EN. Your choice is saved. “Licenses” opens the folder containing dependency licenses. “Report a problem” opens GitHub; your settings are not sent automatically.

Updates

Find “Check for updates” in About and the tray menu. Read “What’s new”, choose “Update now”, “Skip this version” or “Later”. After signature verification, installation closes radio and reopens it.

When a station does not play

Check your internet connection and try another station. radioMAT retries the connection after 10 seconds. Some stations do not provide track titles.
